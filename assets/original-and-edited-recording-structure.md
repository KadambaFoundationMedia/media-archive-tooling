# Example of an original and edited recording

A recording usually has this structure before editing

1\. Om namo bhagavate vasudeva ajam (repeated 3x times)  
2\. We are reading from xxxxx  
3\. Sanskrit Verse Roman ((listeners respond) → can be repeated 2 or 3 times  
4\. One member of the audience is invited to speak the verse individually and the audience will repeat.  
5\. Word for Word (listeners respond)  
6\. English translations  
7\. Purport  
8\. Oblations to the guru (Om ajnana timaranda)  
9\. Explanation of the purport by the speaker. → This is the actual “class”

A class should have this structure after editing:

1. We are reading from  
2. 1x sanskrit verse (roman)  
3. Word by word translation  
4. Translation  
5. Purport  
6. Class (the explanation of the purport by the speaker)  
7. Questions and answers (if present)
